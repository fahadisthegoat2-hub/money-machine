# 7-Day Cash Sprint

Targets: **30 personalized outreach messages, 5 conversations, 2 calls, 1 paid job.**

| Day | Focus |
|---|---|
| 1 | Lock niche + offer (offer-builder). Build 1 sample. |
| 2 | Build list of 40 prospects (name, link, why they need it). |
| 3 | Send 10 personalized DMs/emails. |
| 4 | Send 10 more. Follow up on Day 3. |
| 5 | Send 10 more. Book calls. |
| 6 | Calls/quotes. Send invoice with 50% deposit. |
| 7 | Deliver or start work. Review metrics. Ask for referral/testimonial (real ones only). |

## Message formula
Hook (specific compliment/observation) → problem → 1-line offer → low-friction CTA. Under 60 words.

## Tracking
Keep a simple table: prospect · channel · sent · replied · call · quote · paid. Review: reply rate <10% → fix hook; calls but no close → fix price/offer.

## Rules
No spam blasts, no fake urgency, no misleading claims. Respect platform rules and opt-outs.
